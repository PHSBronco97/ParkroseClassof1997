Welcome to our Five Year Reunion!

 

Tonight's occasion was held on June 13, 2002 just nine days prior to our actual day of graduation.

Gathering at the McMenamins Edgefield Power Station Pub in Troutdale, Oregon, the casual atmosphere called for an informal social setting with folks arriving and leaving as pleased.

Indeed the evening, beginning at seven, brought classmates coming from all areas till the late night hours. Specialty drinks, casual dining, photo shots, and good conversations were the main focuses of the night.

Hosted by the PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee, low cost promotional efforts were concentrated online, via classmates.com, electronic mail, and through verbal communication from alumni. Many thanks to everyone for passing on the reunion announcement!

Thanks for a great Five Year Reunion, and we will see everyone again soon!

Best wishes!
