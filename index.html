We’re the Broncos

We’re the Broncos! Rooted in Portland, Oregon, our class represents a unique journey through the Parkrose School District. Many of us began our educational adventure at Prescott, Sacrament, and other local schools before forming the close-knit community that became the Parkrose High School Class of 1997.

Like every graduating class, we shared the triumphs, challenges, milestones, and unforgettable moments that define the high school experience. We also hold a special place in Parkrose history as part of a class that has continued to support one another across decades.

Today, decades later, the Class of 1997 continues to celebrate a lifetime of experiences, achievements, friendships, and memories. This website serves as both a digital time capsule and a connection point for classmates, families, and friends.

Thank you for visiting, and welcome back to the place where our story began.

Onto the end for Parkrose High!
